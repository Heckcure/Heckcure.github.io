About Heckcure

Security consultancy,
built around partnership.

We exist for a simple reason: most organizations don't get breached because they lack security tools — they get breached because nobody tested the gaps between them the way an attacker would.

Who we are

A consultancy, not a vendor that disappears after the invoice.

Heckcure is a cyber security consultancy focused on offensive testing, defensive hardening, adversary simulation, advisory work, and managed security services. We work as an extension of your team rather than a name that shows up once and vanishes — scoping engagements around your actual environment, communicating in plain language instead of jargon, and staying reachable long after the final report lands in your inbox.

Information security isn't a single product or a one-off test. It's an ongoing relationship between a business and the risks it carries. We bring the technical depth to find what others miss, and the judgement to know what's actually worth fixing first — so your team spends its time on real risk, not noise.

As information security specialists, we cut through the process and oddities that slow real security work down, keeping delivery agile without cutting corners on rigor. Every client gets a service built around how they operate, not a package pulled off a shelf.
